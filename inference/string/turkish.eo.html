1# Turkish locale specializing the default `english` casing with the dotted
2# and dotless I mappings that Turkish and Azeri override the default with.
3# Being a decorator of its string, a `turkish` object behaves like the
4# string itself (`.length`, `.eq` and so on pass through), while adding
5# `lower` and `upper` that honour those mappings. Lower casing maps capital
6# dotless I (U+0049) to small dotless i (U+0131) and capital dotted I
7# (U+0130) to small dotted i (U+0069); upper casing maps small dotted i
8# (U+0069) to capital dotted I (U+0130) and small dotless i (U+0131) to
9# capital dotless I (U+0049). Each direction only touches two of the four
10# characters, which is why `lower` and `upper` list different regexes. The
11# Turkish replacements run before the `english` ASCII pass on purpose: once
12# that pass folds capital dotless I into small dotted i the distinction is
13# gone, so the order must not swap.
