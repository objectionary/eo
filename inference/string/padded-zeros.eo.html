1# Returns the string padded on the left with zeros until it counts `width`
2# characters, keeping a leading minus sign in front of them, so that `-42`
3# padded to five characters becomes `-0042` and not `00-42`. Characters are
4# counted, and not bytes. A string of `width` characters or longer is
5# returned unchanged.
6# If `width` is not a finite non-negative integer (negative, fractional, nan
7# or infinite), the `cant-pad` fallback is returned, or the terminator
8# when unbound.
