1# Writes the number in scientific notation, as a mantissa with six fraction
2# digits, the letter `e`, and the power of ten that scales the mantissa back,
3# so that `1.0e19` becomes `1.000000e19`, and `1e-7` becomes
4# `1.000000e-7`. A magnitude past the range of a signed 64-bit integer is
5# named this way, since `as-decimal` writes only what it spells out digit by
6# digit, and a non-finite number becomes `nan`, `infinity` or `-infinity`,
7# having no digits at all. The mantissa is always normalized to `[1, 10)`, so
8# a magnitude below one carries a negative power, as `5.000000e-1` for `0.5`.
9# A magnitude below the normal range of a `double` is brought up into that range
10# before the mantissa is taken, since the power of ten that would scale it in
11# one step is itself subnormal and carries too few bits to divide by.
