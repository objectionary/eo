1# Calculate MOD.
2# An operation that finds the remainder after dividing `x` by `y`. When `y`
3# is zero, the result is the caller-supplied `cant-mod` fallback, or the
4# terminator when none was provided, which terminates on use. Otherwise
5# the divisor is doubled until it reaches the dividend and then halved back
6# down, subtracting it from the dividend whenever it fits. Doubling and
7# halving a double are exact, and every subtraction happens while the
8# remainder is below twice the subtracted value, which is exact too, so the
9# answer matches the true remainder for any pair of finite numbers, however
10# far apart their magnitudes are, at the cost of two recursive steps per
11# bit of the quotient. When the dividend is `nan` or infinite, the result is
12# `nan`, regardless of the divisor.
