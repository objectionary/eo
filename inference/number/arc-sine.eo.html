1# Arc sine of `num` as `org.eolang.number`, the angle in radians whose sine is
2# `num`. An argument above one half is folded toward zero through the identity
3# asin(x) equals pi/2 minus twice asin(sqrt((1 minus x) div 2)), so the Taylor
4# series always runs in its fast region, while arguments outside [-1, 1] and
5# non-finite ones collapse to nan.
