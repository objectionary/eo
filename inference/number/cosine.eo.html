1# Cosine of the angle in radians as `org.eolang.number`, taken from the sine
2# of the half angle, since cos(x) equals one less twice the square of
3# sin(x/2).
4#
5# The obvious route, sin(x + pi/2), cannot be taken: a quarter turn added to
6# a large angle is lost in the rounding of the sum, and at `1e15`, where a
7# double steps by an eighth, the shift moves the argument by more than the
8# answer is worth. Halving an angle is exact for every double, so the half
9# angle route hands `sine` a number it can reduce as accurately as the
10# original.
11#
12# The price is the square: where the cosine is near zero the two terms of the
13# subtraction are near equal and their leading digits cancel, so the answer
14# keeps its absolute accuracy and not its relative one. Zero is not such a
15# place: sin(0) is exactly zero, so the unit value comes out exact without
16# being asked for.
