1# Writes the number as signed decimal digits, truncating its fraction
2# towards zero, so that `42.7` becomes `42` and `-42.7` becomes `-42`.
3#
4# The digits are peeled off an `i64`, where division by ten is exact, so every
5# magnitude below `2^63` is written exactly, and not only the magnitudes below
6# `2^53` that a double divides without losing a digit.
7#
8# The sign of an exact negative zero is kept, as `as-fixed` and `as-scientific`
9# keep it, so `-0.0` becomes `-0` and not `0`. A negative number whose fraction
10# is truncated away loses its sign, since `-0.5` is written as the `0` it
11# truncates to.
