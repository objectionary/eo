1# Writes the number `n` as signed decimal digits, truncating its fraction
2# towards zero, so that `42.7` becomes `42` and `-42.7` becomes `-42`.
3#
4# The digits are peeled off an `i64`, where division by ten is exact, so every
5# magnitude below `2^63` is written exactly, and not only the magnitudes below
6# `2^53` that a double divides without losing a digit.
