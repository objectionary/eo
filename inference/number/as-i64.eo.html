1# Converts the number to an `i64`, truncating the fractional part toward
2# zero. The exponent and the mantissa are pulled out of its IEEE 754 layout
3# and the magnitude is shifted into place, with the two's complement taken
4# when it is negative. When it does not fit into the bounds of an `i64`,
5# the caller-supplied `cant-convert` fallback is returned.
