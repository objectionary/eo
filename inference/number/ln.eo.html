1# Natural logarithm of `num` with base `e` as `org.eolang.number`. The argument
2# is folded into [1/e, e] by peeling powers of `e` that double at each step
3# (e, e^2, e^4, ..., e^512), so the depth is bounded regardless of how large
4# the argument is, then the remainder is summed with the area-hyperbolic-
5# tangent series. The limiting cases follow the usual conventions: zero is
6# negative infinity, a negative number and negative infinity are nan, and
7# positive infinity stays.
