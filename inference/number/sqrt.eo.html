1# Positive square root of `num` as `org.eolang.number`, taken as `num` raised to
2# the one-half power. A negative argument, negative infinity included, is nan.
3#
4# The half power goes through a logarithm and an exponential, both of which
5# lose digits as the exponent of the receiver grows, so at `1e300` only
6# fourteen of the sixteen digits a double holds are right. One Heron step
7# takes that seed to the last place: the step squares the relative error, and
8# an error of `1e-14` squared is far below what a double can tell apart. A
9# seed that is zero, infinite or nan has nothing to polish and is the answer
10# already.
