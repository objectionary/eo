1# Sine of `num` radians as `org.eolang.number`.
2# The argument is reduced into the [-pi, pi] range with a Cody-Waite style
3# two-part reduction constant, and the Taylor series is summed through
4# Horner's scheme. The high part of that constant has 26 trailing zero
5# mantissa bits, so `k times tau_hi` stays exact only while `k` is under
6# `2^26`, which puts the reduction's few-ULP accuracy at |num| up to about
7# 4e8; past that point the product itself rounds and the error lands
8# straight in the reduced angle. Non-finite arguments collapse to nan.
