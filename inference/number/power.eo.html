1# Raises the number to the power of `b` as `org.eolang.number`. A zero exponent
2# gives one, an integer exponent is expanded by exponentiation-by-squaring so
3# integer results stay exact, and a fractional exponent of a positive base is
4# taken as e raised to `b` times the natural logarithm of the base. Zeros,
5# infinities and nan follow the same IEEE rules that Math.pow obeys.
