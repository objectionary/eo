1# Integer division of an `i64` number by the `i64` number `x`, truncated
2# toward zero. The quotient is built by restoring long division over the
3# magnitudes of both operands, with the sign applied afterwards. When `x` is
4# zero, the caller-supplied `cant-divide` fallback is returned, and so it is
5# when the smallest `i64` is divided by minus one, since that quotient is one
6# past the largest `i64` and has no answer here.
