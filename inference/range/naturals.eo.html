1# Range of natural numbers from `start` to `end` (soft border) with step = 1.
2# It is a method of `range`, so it reads `start` and `end` from the range it
3# is taken from. Both bounds must be integers, otherwise the computation
4# terminates: a fractional or non-finite seed would otherwise stay fractional
5# or non-finite through the whole generated sequence, contradicting the range
6# of natural numbers. `is-integer` already excludes `nan` and the infinities,
7# so it doubles as the finiteness check.
