1# Writes the bytes as uppercase hexadecimal pairs joined by a hyphen,
2# so that the two bytes `0x4A 0x65` become `4A-65`.
3#
4# Each nibble is read as a number through the exponent pattern of `2^52`,
5# a double with that exponent and a mantissa `m` being exactly `2^52 + m`,
6# and then picks its digit out of a sixteen-character table.
