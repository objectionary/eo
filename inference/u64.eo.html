1# Unsigned 64-bit integer.
2# Here `as-bytes` must be a `bytes` object of exactly eight bytes,
3# interpreted without a sign. Addition, subtraction and multiplication
4# share the bit pattern of their signed `i64` counterparts, while
5# comparisons and division are made unsigned.
6# `as-number` rounds exactly once. Above `2^63` the eight bytes are
7# shifted right by one bit, the bit that falls off is put back as the
8# lowest bit of the shifted value, and the result is doubled. That
9# lowest bit keeps the shifted value odd whenever something was
10# discarded, so it can never sit on a tie and be rounded the wrong way.
